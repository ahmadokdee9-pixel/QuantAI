# Trust Framework

Trust is not a brand adjective for QuantAI.  
**Trust is the product constraint that makes Decision Intelligence possible.**

Without trust, calibration is ignored, records are theater, and the company becomes another persuasive interface.

---

## Trust thesis

People will only outsource judgment to software that is:

1. **Consistent** — same decision everywhere  
2. **Honest under uncertainty** — quiet when evidence is weak  
3. **Resistant to capture** — not for sale to the highest merchant  
4. **Explainable enough** — reasons match the real engine path  
5. **Improving visibly** — fewer repeated mistakes over time  

QuantAI optimizes for these properties even when they hurt short-term conversion.

---

## Layers of trust

### 1. Epistemic trust — “Is this true enough to act?”

- Credible discounts only
- Trust and review signals treated as adversarial
- Hard mismatch gates over soft score cosmetics
- Degraded modes that admit upstream failure instead of inventing certainty

### 2. Procedural trust — “Did you decide the way you claim?”

- One ranking authority
- Calibration after rank, not a shadow re-rank
- Ranking Decision Records aligned to the real path
- No second ad-hoc ranker in the client for default judgment

### 3. Relational trust — “Are you on my side?”

- Agent-aligned incentives (user outcome > placement revenue)
- Refusal of domains and actions that require betrayal
- Continuity that helps waiting, not only buying
- Plans that expand power without paywalling basic honesty

### 4. Institutional trust — “Will you still be trustworthy at scale?”

- Regression locks on decision semantics
- Gated learning writebacks
- Clear LIVE vs research boundaries in claims
- Public humility about dependencies and limits

## Confidence policy

Confidence is a promise. Policy:

- High confidence requires strong evidence and constraint fit  
- Medium confidence invites compare  
- Low confidence should widen alternatives or recommend wait  
- Unknown must be sayable  

A tray flooded with high-confidence BUY labels is a trust incident, not a win.

---

## Explainability standard

Explanations must be:

- **Faithful** — derived from the decision path, not a plausible story
- **Selective** — few reasons that mattered, not fifty scores
- **Actionable** — what would change the decision
- **Humble** — explicit about missing evidence

Copilot language inherits this standard. Fluency without fidelity is a defect.

---

## Incentive integrity

Long-term monetization must pass the Trust Test:

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|
| Subscriptions for capacity/depth | Selling rank to merchants |
| B2B Decision API with disclosed contracts | Secret placement bias |
| Affiliate as secondary, disclosed, never overriding authority | “Partner boost” inside calibration |
| Enterprise embeds that preserve engine law | White-label that silently corrupts gates |

If revenue requires lying with labels, the revenue is rejected.

---

## Trust and the Decision Graph

The graph increases power; power needs policy:

- Consent and minimization for personal nodes  
- Aggregate learning preferred for global priors  
- No resale of vulnerability profiles  
- User-visible control over memory where feasible  
- Security of decision history as sensitive data  

Breach of decision history is breach of identity-as-chooser. Treat it accordingly.

---

## Trust in multi-domain expansion

Entering a new domain requires a trust checklist:

1. Can we be agent-aligned?  
2. Can we refuse when evidence is weak?  
3. Can outcomes be learned without harming users?  
4. Are we accidentally becoming a regulated advisor of record?  
5. Will failure modes damage trust in commerce (the wedge)?  

If expansion risks the wedge’s trust, expansion waits.

---

## Communicating trust externally

Rules for claims:

- Do not market dormant systems as live  
- Do not claim inventory or data we do not own  
- Do not imply infallibility  
- Do claim: calibrated decisions, authority discipline, credibility gates, auditability  

Understatement is a competitive advantage in a market drowning in AI exaggeration.

---

## Trust failure modes to watch

| Failure | Symptom |
|---------|---------|
| Capture | Leaders suspiciously correlate with commercial relationships |
| Theater | Discount chips without evidence |
| Divergence | Compare/chat disagree with grid |
| Overconfidence | BUY inflation |
| Opacity | Reasons that cannot be traced to records |
| Addiction product | Engagement without open decisions |

Each failure has an owner: product, engine, or growth. None are “marketing problems.”

---

## Founder standard

**We would rather lose a purchase than win it with a dishonest decision.**

That sentence is the Trust Framework in one line. Keep it true for ten years and QuantAI can become infrastructure. Break it once at scale and the category dies with us.
