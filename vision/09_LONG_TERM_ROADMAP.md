# Long-Term Roadmap

Horizon: **10 years.**  
Objective: evolve QuantAI from a commerce decision product into the **operating system for Decision Intelligence** — without abandoning the laws that make the wedge trustworthy.

This is a company roadmap, not a sprint board. Dates are eras, not promises.

---

## North-star sequence

```text
Product Decisions (wedge)
        → Deep commerce mastery + compounding graph
                → Adjacent economic decisions
                        → Decision platform / API
                                → Universal Decision Intelligence
```

Each era must leave the Decision Engine more authoritative, not more theatrical.

---

## Era 0 — Prove the wedge (years 0–1)

**Goal:** Become the most trusted way to decide what to buy, where, and whether to wait — for a real user base in initial markets.

**Build**

- Harden live decision core (authority, calibration, credibility, diversity)
- Latency and reliability worthy of a daily habit
- Continuity: save, watch, alert, revisit under price/availability change
- Outcome instrumentation foundations (consented, privacy-aware)
- Honest positioning as Decision Intelligence — not “another shopping site”

**Do not**

- Rewrite ranking religion for novelty
- Turn on the entire dormant estate for marketing
- Launch a marketplace
- Expand to five verticals shallowly

**Exit criteria**

- Users return for open decisions, not only cold search
- Decision quality measurable against naive sort/chat baselines
- Trust mechanics intact under growth pressure

---

## Era 1 — Compound the graph (years 1–3)

**Goal:** Turn seeds (observations, records, trust priors, history) into a real Decision Graph with gated learning.

**Build**

- Unified episode → decision → outcome model
- Offline learning loops for trust, value, timing, calibration
- Stronger identity resolution across offers
- Category depth in high-consideration commerce packs
- Distribution that preserves incentive integrity

**Proprietary assets that should become obvious**

- Outcome-linked calibration corpus
- Source trust that reflects reality
- Timing intelligence that ages well

**Exit criteria**

- Clear evidence that more usage improves decision quality
- Graph density high enough that a clone of the UI without the corpus feels dumber

---

## Era 2 — Adjacent domains + platform spike (years 3–6)

**Goal:** Prove the engine is domain-portable; begin platformization.

**Build**

- One adjacent economic domain that reuses timing/trust/calibration laws
- Decision API (rank + calibrate + records) for selected partners
- Domain pack architecture (shared engine, local laws)
- Embed path for strategic surfaces

**Guardrails**

- No domain that violates the never-support list
- No partner contract that sells placement inside authority
- Commerce wedge trust remains sacred

**Exit criteria**

- Same engine contract serving ≥2 domains
- External client depending on Decision API in production
- Pack model prevents codebase forks

---

## Era 3 — Universal Decision Intelligence (years 6–10)

**Goal:** QuantAI is recognized as infrastructure for judgment on the internet.

**Build**

- Multi-domain OS with strict eligibility law
- Global graph priors with local personalization boundaries
- Institutional / enterprise decision layers where incentives align
- Developer ecosystem for domain packs under QuantAI law
- Geographic depth where decision quality can be honest

**Company shape**

- Consumer habit in commerce still matters (soul of the brand)
- Platform revenue can become primary without corrupting labels
- Research continues, but LIVE claims stay gated

**Exit criteria**

- “Ask QuantAI” means “get a calibrated decision,” not “search products”
- Switching costs are graph + trust + workflow, not UI lock-in
- Category language (Decision Intelligence) is associated with QuantAI’s standard

## What compounds every era

| Asset | Compounding role |
|-------|------------------|
| Decision Engine semantics | Stability attracts trust and partners |
| Decision Graph | Data moat |
| Regression / gate culture | Quality as institutional memory |
| Trust / incentive integrity | Permission to expand |
| Domain packs | Distribution without identity crisis |

---

## Network effects timeline (realistic)

| Era | Effect strength | Source |
|-----|-----------------|--------|
| 0 | Low | Mostly product quality, not networks |
| 1 | Emerging | Outcome density, trust density |
| 2 | Medium | Cross-client API volume + consumer corpus |
| 3 | Strong | Platform + multi-domain graph |

Do not claim Era-3 effects in Era-0 marketing.

---

## What gets smarter

Industry language models will improve on their own. QuantAI’s unique path is **gated, decision-grade learning from resolved choices** — calibration, trust priors, value authenticity, timing, intent binding, and domain failure predictors. Detail lives in the Decision Engine and Decision Graph docs; the roadmap only requires that learning stay shadowed, measurable, and reversible.

---

## Explicit non-roadmap

- Becoming a merchant of record
- Ad-funded ranking
- Unlimited vertical splash launches
- AGI theater as company strategy
- Replacing regulated professionals as authority of record
- UI redesign as a substitute for decision quality

---

## Operating cadence across all eras

1. Freeze decision semantics before growth stunts  
2. Activate intelligence only behind gates and shadows  
3. Prefer depth over novelty  
4. Measure decision quality, not only engagement  
5. Expand domains only when the eligibility law passes  

---

## One-line roadmap

**Earn the right to decide purchases → remember how the world chose → open the engine as infrastructure → become the OS for decisions.**
