# Decision Intelligence

## Category definition

**Decision Intelligence** is the discipline of turning options, constraints, and uncertain signals into a calibrated action — with authority, confidence, and memory — at machine scale.

It sits between:

| Upstream | Downstream |
|----------|------------|
| Search, feeds, catalogs, market data | Checkout, booking, commitment, execution |

Search answers: *What exists?*  
Decision Intelligence answers: *What should I do?*

---

## Why Decision Intelligence is a new category

Existing categories fail the forever problem in different ways:

| Category | What it optimizes | Where it breaks |
|----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Search engines | Retrieval relevance | Stops before judgment |
| Marketplaces | Transaction completion | Biased by inventory & ads |
| Comparison sites | Attribute tables | Tables ≠ decisions |
| Deal aggregators | Discount surface area | Discount theater wins |
| Chatbots / copilots | Fluent assistance | Fluency ≠ calibrated choice |
| Recommenders | Engagement / affinity | Often opaque and seller-aligned |

Decision Intelligence is none of these. It is a **judgment system**: rank, gate, calibrate, explain, learn from outcomes.

QuantAI’s claim is not “we added AI to shopping.” It is: **we are building the category of Decision Intelligence**, starting where economic decisions are densest.

---

## The Decision Intelligence stack

```text
Intent        →  what the human is trying to achieve
Options       →  candidates from the open world
Signals       →  price, trust, quality, timing, fit, risk
Authority     →  single ranking law
Calibration   →  action labels + confidence
Explanation   →  decision records humans can audit
Continuity    →  memory across time
Outcomes      →  what happened after the decision
Learning      →  priors and models that improve
```

Remove any layer and the system collapses into search, chat, or ads.

---

## Properties of a Decision Intelligence system

A system qualifies as Decision Intelligence only if it has all of the following:

1. **Canonical authority** — one ordering law, shared across surfaces  
2. **Action calibration** — outputs are decisions (act / compare / avoid / wait), not only scores  
3. **Mismatch discipline** — hard constraints can demote or veto leaders  
4. **Credibility gates** — adversarial or weak signals cannot buy influence  
5. **Explainable records** — every material ranking can be reconstructed  
6. **Outcome orientation** — success is decision quality over time, not clickbait  
7. **Domain packs, shared engine** — new domains add laws, not a new company identity  

These properties are the category’s constitution. Features that do not serve them are distractions.

## Why QuantAI can define the category

Faster search will never remove judgment. QuantAI already runs the nucleus of Decision Intelligence in commerce: open-world option intake, signal enrichment, canonical authority, calibrated actions, credibility gates, and decision records.

That nucleus is the seed of the category — not a shopping gimmick. The path is: **name the category honestly, prove it in one domain, generalize the stack without diluting authority.**

---

## Decision Intelligence vs artificial general intelligence

AGI aspires to broad cognition. Decision Intelligence aspires to **reliable choice under constraints**.

QuantAI does not need to know everything. It needs to be the best system in the world at:

- Structuring a decision
- Ranking under uncertainty
- Saying no when evidence is weak
- Improving after outcomes

This is a narrower, more defensible, more productizable ambition than “be an AI that chats about products.”

## Economic meaning of the category

Whoever owns Decision Intelligence owns:

- The moment before money moves
- The moment before commitment locks
- The trust layer platforms will rent or embed
- The data exhaust of resolved choices (with consent and care)

That is why this can become a global technology company: **decisions are upstream of nearly every internet transaction.**

---

## Category non-goals

Decision Intelligence is not:

- Replacing human moral agency on life-critical choices
- Becoming the merchant of record
- Maximizing affiliate yield over user outcomes
- Generating infinite content about options

It is judgment infrastructure. It must stay that.
