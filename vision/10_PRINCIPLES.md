# Principles

The constitution of QuantAI.  
If a proposal violates these, it does not ship — regardless of upside.

---

## 1. Decisions are the product

We exist to improve judgment under uncertainty.  
Features that do not improve calibrated decisions are optional. Features that harm them are forbidden.

## 2. One authority

There is one canonical decision order for a given intent and option set.  
Surfaces may explain and explore. They may not secretly re-rank the default judgment.

## 3. Truth before language

Deterministic decision law outranks generative fluency.  
Models may assist; they may not become the unaccountable ranker.

## 4. Calibration honesty

Labels and confidence must be earned.  
`AVOID`, `COMPARE`, and wait are first-class successes when they prevent regret. False BUY inflation is a defect.

## 5. Credibility over theater

Adversarial signals (fake discounts, spam trust, paid vibes) do not purchase influence.  
When evidence is weak, we get quieter.

## 6. Agent alignment

We optimize for the decision-maker’s outcome.  
Placement revenue, partnerships, and growth tactics lose when they conflict with judgment quality.

## 7. Alternatives remain real

A decision without preserved alternatives is a storefront.  
Diversity and option freedom are structural, not cosmetic.

## 8. Explainability is owed

Material decisions emit faithful records.  
Reasons must match the path that produced the order.

## 9. Continuity respects time

Prices, availability, and constraints change.  
Holding a decision open (save / watch / revisit) is core product behavior.

## 10. Learning must be gated

Outcome learning compounds the company only if it is shadowed, measurable, and reversible.  
Ungated “smartness” near authority is banned.

## 11. The wedge is sacred

Product Decisions prove we deserve to exist.  
No expansion may cannibalize trust in the wedge for narrative ambition.

## 12. Domain restraint

We support decisions that fit ranking, calibration, trust, and outcome learning.  
We refuse authority over life-critical regulated judgment, civic manipulation, harm markets, and any domain that requires us to lie.

## 13. Platform without corruption

APIs and embeds must carry engine law with them.  
White-label that sells fake decisions is not distribution — it is brand suicide.

## 14. Underclaim in public

LIVE is LIVE. Research is research.  
We do not market dormant systems, owned inventory we lack, or infallibility.

## 15. Depth over theater

Fewer domains, done with institutional quality, beat a zoo of demos.  
More engines ≠ better decisions.

## 16. Long-term compounding

We prefer assets that get more valuable with use: Decision Engine semantics, Decision Graph, trust priors, regression culture.  
We reject growth that burns those assets for a quarter of vanity metrics.

---

## Conflict resolution

When principles collide in practice, resolve in this order:

1. Human safety and legal/moral red lines  
2. Agent alignment / non-capture  
3. Authority consistency & calibration honesty  
4. Wedge trust  
5. Learning and expansion ambitions  
6. Revenue tactics  

---

## The short form

**Decide truthfully. Stay uncaptured. Compound judgment. Refuse what corrupts.**

That is QuantAI.
