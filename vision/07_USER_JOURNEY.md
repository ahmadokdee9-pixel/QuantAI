# User Journey

QuantAI’s journey is not a funnel to checkout.  
It is a **relationship with unfinished and recurring decisions.**

---

## The job to be done

When a person faces option overload under constraints, they hire QuantAI to:

1. Narrow chaos into a leader and real alternatives  
2. Name the action (buy / compare / avoid / wait)  
3. Explain enough to trust the call  
4. Hold the decision open when timing is wrong  
5. Remember preferences so the next episode is faster  

If the product only “shows results,” the job is incomplete.

---

## Journey stages (commerce wedge)

### 1. Arrival with intent

The user arrives with a messy goal: a product class, a budget, a brand, a fear of buying wrong.

QuantAI’s first job is **intent binding** — turn language into a decidable problem — without interrogating them to death.

### 2. Decision surface, not catalog tour

Within one motion they should see:

- A canonical leader
- Calibrated actions
- Why the leader won
- Alternatives that remain alive
- Discount/trust claims only when credible

This is the first trust test. Latency and honesty matter more than feature count.

### 3. Interrogation of the judgment

Power users open compare, drawer intelligence, or copilot. These surfaces must **orbit the same authority**, not invent a rival ranking.

Healthy behavior: “Convince me / show me tradeoffs.”  
Unhealthy behavior: “Ignore your own leader because the chatbot liked something else.”

### 4. Commitment or continuity

Two successful endings:

| Ending | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Act** | Outbound to merchant with clear eyes |
| **Continue** | Save, watch, alert, revisit — timing not ready |

Wait is not bounce. Wait is product.

### 5. Return under change

Prices move. Stock dies. New offers appear. Constraints shift.

The daily/weekly return loop is:

**Something in my open decisions changed — what should I do now?**

That loop is why QuantAI can become habitual without becoming a social feed.

### 6. Preference compounding

Over time, the product should need less re-explanation:

- Markets and languages used
- Trust sensitivity
- Budget bands
- Category tastes
- Hard exclusions

Personal context accelerates judgment. It must never trap the user in a filter bubble that hides better options.

---

## Why users return every day

Daily return requires **open decision inventory**, not novelty content.

| Return reason | Mechanism |
|---------------|-----------|
| Price movement on watched items | Alerts / watchlist |
| Timing questions (“buy now or wait?”) | Re-decision on same intent |
| New needs in high-frequency categories | Fast path from memory |
| Distrust of merchant theater elsewhere | Habit of checking QuantAI before paying |
| Compare-before-commit ritual | Saved trays and history |

The long-term consumer habit: **QuantAI is the last stop before money moves.**

---

## Journey evolution (10-year)

| Era | User relationship |
|-----|-------------------|
| **Years 0–2** | “Helps me buy better” |
| **Years 2–5** | “Holds my economic decisions over time” |
| **Years 5–10** | “My decision OS — commerce first, then eligible domains” |

The emotional contract stays constant: **calm, precise, high-trust judgment.**  
Plans may expand capacity and depth; they must not paywall basic honesty or sell fake confidence.

---

## Moments that create or destroy trust

| Creates trust | Destroys trust |
|---------------|----------------|
| Rare, earned BUY labels | Everything marked urgent |
| Clear AVOID with reason | Silent bad leaders |
| Stable order across compare | Chat contradicts the grid |
| Honest “evidence weak” | Fake completeness under upstream failure |
| Fast warm revisits | Cold multi-second emptiness as the brand feeling |
| Wait recommendations that age well | Pushing purchase for metrics |

Trust compounds slower than growth hacks — and outlasts them.

---

## From single session to life of decisions

A mature QuantAI user does not “search more.” They maintain a **portfolio of decisions**:

- Active purchases under consideration
- Watches waiting for price truth
- Categories where taste is known
- Domains later added under the same OS

The journey’s end state is not a completed onboarding checklist.  
It is: **QuantAI becomes how they choose.**

---

## Journey non-goals

- Infinite scroll engagement
- Gamified streaks for shopping
- Pushing users to open the app without an open decision
- Replacing the human at the moment of moral or life-critical choice

We want presence at decision time — not addiction between decisions.
