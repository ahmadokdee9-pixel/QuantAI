# Decision Domains

QuantAI will not support every decision humans make.  
It will support **decisions that share the laws of ranking, calibration, trust, and outcomes** — and refuse domains that corrupt the mission.

---

## Domain selection law

A domain is eligible only if most of these are true:

1. Options are plural and comparable  
2. Signals are noisy, adversarial, or incomplete  
3. Mistakes have economic or practical cost  
4. Outcomes can be observed or reasonably inferred  
5. A calibrated action (act / compare / avoid / wait) is meaningful  
6. QuantAI can remain agent-aligned (not seller-captured)  
7. Failure does not require QuantAI to assume regulated professional liability it cannot carry  

If a domain fails this law, it is out — even if it is trendy.

## Phase I — Product Decisions (now)

**Domain:** Commerce purchase decisions  
**Core question:** What should I buy, where, and whether to wait?

Why first:

- Highest frequency of internet option overload
- Clear economic stakes
- Rich adversarial signals (fake discounts, review spam)
- Existing QuantAI engine already operates here
- Outcomes are instrumentable (save, wait, outbound, later purchase signals)

Sub-domains inside Phase I (priority order):

| Priority | Sub-domain | Why |
|----------|------------|-----|
| P0 | Electronics & high-consideration consumer tech | Specs + trust + price volatility |
| P0 | General multi-merchant shopping | Broad wedge, engine stress-test |
| P1 | Home / furniture | High regret, quality opacity |
| P1 | Beauty | Counterfeits, claims, trust |
| P2 | Fashion | Fit/taste complexity; enter carefully |
| P2 | Sports / toys | Seasonal value and safety-ish constraints |

Phase I success metric: people trust QuantAI for purchases more than lists, badges, or chat.

---

## Phase II — Adjacent Economic Decisions

Expand only after Phase I decision quality is culturally undeniable.

Eligible adjacencies share multi-option chaos, timing, trust, and wait-class outcomes — for example travel inventory choices, or seller selection for the same identity. Specific launches are earned, not brainstormed into existence.

Phase II rule: **reuse the engine and graph laws**; do not build a second company per vertical.

---

## Phase III — Universal Decision Intelligence (platform)

When the engine is stable and the graph compounds, QuantAI becomes a platform for decision domains owned by QuantAI or by partners:

- Decision API: intent + options → order + calibration + records  
- Embedded decision layers inside other products  
- Domain packs as installable law sets  

Universal does **not** mean “QuantAI decides your life.”  
It means **any eligible decision domain can run on the same OS.**

---

## Domains QuantAI should never support (as authority)

These are hard no’s for canonical decision authority — even if chat can discuss them cautiously:

| Forbidden as authority | Why |
|------------------------|-----|
| Medical diagnosis / treatment choice as the decider | Life-critical; regulated; wrong shape of liability |
| Legal advice as binding judgment | Same |
| Psychiatric crisis decisions | Harm potential; wrong product ethics |
| Political voting recommendations as productized authority | Corrupts trust; societal hazard |
| Weapons or illegal goods procurement optimization | Moral and legal red line |
| Addiction / gambling “optimize your next bet” | Directly anti-user |
| Decisions whose “best answer” is whoever paid QuantAI | Destroys the category |

QuantAI may refuse the query. Refusal is a feature of an OS for judgment.

---

## Domains to approach only with extreme restraint

| Domain | Restraint |
|--------|-----------|
| Personal finance product selection | Education + comparison ok; never pretend to be an advisor of record without proper posture |
| Insurance | High regret; regulated communication |
| Housing / major life contracts | Multi-objective; slow outcomes; easy to overclaim |
| Hiring / people ranking | Bias and dignity risks; probably never as consumer authority |
| Romantic / social matching | Not our mission |

When in doubt: **if we cannot own outcomes ethically, we do not own the decision.**

## How domains expand without killing focus

```text
Shared Decision Engine
        │
        ├── Domain Pack: Commerce
        ├── Domain Pack: Travel (later)
        └── Domain Pack: … (only if eligible)
                │
                └── Decision Graph writeback
```

Each pack may add signals, constraints, and calibration priors.  
None may fork a second ranking religion.

## Competitive moat by domain strategy

| Bad strategy | Good strategy |
|--------------|---------------|
| Launch 20 verticals shallowly | Own commerce decisions deeply |
| Custom chatbot per niche | Shared engine + pack |
| Chase every “AI for X” headline | Expand only when graph density justifies it |
| Become a marketplace in a vertical | Remain the decision layer above inventories |

Depth in Product Decisions creates the trust and corpus that later domains rent.

---

## First / never — founder summary

**Support first:** Product purchase decisions with multi-merchant truth.  
**Support next:** Adjacent economic choices that reuse timing, trust, and calibration.  
**Support eventually:** Any eligible domain via platform packs.  
**Never support as authority:** Life-critical regulated judgment, civic manipulation, harm markets, or paid placement disguised as decision quality.
