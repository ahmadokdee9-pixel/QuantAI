# 10. Market Size

**Method:** Directional TAM/SAM/SOM framing for diligence narrative.  
**Not** a formal third-party market study. Buyers should substitute their own research for valuation modeling.

---

## Problem market

Consumers and assistants need help choosing **what to buy, where, and whether a discount is real** across fragmented merchant offers.

Adjacent markets:

- Online comparison shopping  
- Affiliate / performance commerce media  
- Shopping assistants / AI commerce UX  
- Retail media & recommendation middleware  

---

## TAM / SAM / SOM (illustrative)

| Layer | Definition | Directional view |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| **TAM** | Global online comparison + shopping-assistant + affiliate decision tooling spend | Large (tens of billions USD across overlapping categories) |
| **SAM** | English/EU-capable multi-merchant decision apps and embeddable decision APIs | Material subset of TAM; competitive and distribution-heavy |
| **SOM** | Near-term reachable: niche consumer SaaS + partner embeds in selected geos/categories | Early-stage; **not quantified here with fake precision** |

QuantAI’s realistic near-term beachhead is **decision-quality differentiation** in a crowded shopping UX market—not capturing Google Shopping share.

---

## Demand drivers

- SKU and offer overload across merchants  
- Fake / misleading discount fatigue  
- Rise of AI assistants that still lack commerce ranking discipline  
- Affiliate economics rewarding qualified intent  

---

## Constraints on market capture

| Constraint | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| Upstream discovery dependency | Quality/latency inherit from SerpAPI (or successor) |
| No owned inventory | Cannot win on catalog exclusivity |
| Brand/distribution | Required for consumer scale |
| Trust & compliance | Affiliate disclosures, vendor ToS, privacy |

---

## Buyer takeaway

Market **existence** is not the question — shopping decision pain is real.  
Market **capture** depends on distribution, latency, and monetization packaging more than on inventing another search box.
