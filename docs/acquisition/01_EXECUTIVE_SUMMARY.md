---

## What QuantAI is

QuantAI helps shoppers turn a product search into a **calibrated buying decision**.

A user enters a query. QuantAI:

1. Discovers multi-merchant offers from external commerce sources  
2. Enriches products with trust, value, and relevance signals  
3. Applies **Phase A canonical ranking** (single order authority)  
4. Applies **decision calibration** (BUY / STRONG BUY / COMPARE / BEST VALUE / AVOID)  
5. Presents merchant-diverse results with verified-discount emphasis when evidence is credible  
6. Supports compare, save, and shopper continuity flows  

QuantAI is **software and decision IP**. It does **not** own retailer inventory or a proprietary global product catalog.

---

## Why it matters

Most shopping experiences list or sort products. QuantAI’s differentiation is a **locked decision system**:

- One canonical ranking authority across results grid, decision brief, and compare  
- Calibrated shopper labels with mismatch discipline  
- Credible-only discount presentation  
- Cross-merchant preservation and diversity safeguards  
- Truth / SKU / price-observation foundations  
- Production stabilization for slow upstream feeds  
- Offline regression gates on the decision core
