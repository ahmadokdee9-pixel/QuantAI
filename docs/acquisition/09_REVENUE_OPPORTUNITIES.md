# 9. Revenue Opportunities

**Disclaimer:** Opportunities below are strategic options for an acquirer. They are **not** historical revenue claims.

---

## Near-term (product already oriented)

| Opportunity | Mechanism | Dependency |
|-------------|-----------|------------|
| Subscription upgrades | Free → Pro / Premium via Stripe | Working billing env + conversion UX |
| Retention features | Saved, alerts, memory driving paid necessity | Auth + Supabase reliability |
| Demo-led sales | Guided buyer/partner demos of decision quality | Warm staging + latency control |

---

## Medium-term (packaging / GTM)

| Opportunity | Mechanism | Notes |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Affiliate / partner economics | Monetize outbound clicks where compliant | Requires partner programs + disclosure |
| Vertical landing experiences | Electronics, furniture, etc. decision hubs | Mostly packaging on existing core |
| Content + SEO acquisition | Capture high-intent shopping queries | Needs content ops; product already decision-shaped |
| Controlled intelligence activation | Paid “advanced intelligence” differentiation | Only with gates; avoid overclaim |

---

## Longer-term (platform)

| Opportunity | Mechanism | Notes |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Decision API / white-label | Expose rank + calibrate as service | Requires productization & SLAs |
| OEM / embed into retailer or fintech apps | Strategic integration | Best for strategic acquirers |
| Data products | Aggregated anonymized decision insights | Privacy, ToS, and quality constraints apply |
| Alternate discovery sources | Reduce SerpAPI concentration risk | Engineering investment |

---

## What not to underwrite

- GMV take-rate as if QuantAI were a marketplace  
- Revenue from dormant engines as if already live  
- Exclusive inventory licensing income  

---

## Diligence framing

Revenue upside is real **if** the buyer has distribution or GTM capacity.  
Without distribution, QuantAI remains primarily an **IP + product asset** until growth is built.
