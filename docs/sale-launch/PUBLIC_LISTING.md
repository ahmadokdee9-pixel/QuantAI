## Full product description

QuantAI helps people decide **what to buy and why** when online shopping offers are noisy, inconsistent, and full of weak promotions.

### Problem

Shoppers face:

- Too many similar products across merchants  
- Conflicting “best” rankings across surfaces  
- Fake or weak discount signals  
- Comparison tools that list items without a coherent decision  

Most shopping UIs sort or badge listings. Few enforce a single decision authority end to end.

### Solution

QuantAI is a **commerce decision layer** on top of external product discovery:

1. Discover multi-merchant offers  
2. Enrich with trust, value, and relevance signals  
3. Apply canonical ranking (one order authority)  
4. Apply decision calibration (actionable labels + confidence)  
5. Present results with merchant diversity and credible-discount handling  
6. Support compare, save, and account continuity  

QuantAI does **not** own a global product catalog. Differentiation is the **decision system**, not inventory exclusivity.

### Key product capabilities

- Multi-merchant commerce search  
- Canonical product ranking across results surfaces  
- Calibrated labels: BUY / STRONG BUY / COMPARE / BEST VALUE / AVOID  
- Merchant diversity safeguards  
- Credible discount presentation when evidence supports it  
- Product comparison flow  
- Saved products / continuity (authenticated)  
- Auth and subscription billing foundations  
- Production-oriented stabilization patterns for slow upstream feeds
