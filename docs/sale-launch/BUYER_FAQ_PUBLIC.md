# Buyer FAQ — Public

**Disclosure level:** Public (Level 1)  
**Tone:** Direct. Prefer “unknown / under NDA” over invention.

---

### 1. Is it generating revenue?
**No verified revenue is claimed.** QuantAI is listed as **pre-revenue**. Subscription billing foundations exist in the product, but this public kit does not assert ARR/MRR or sales history.

### 2. Are there active users?
**No verified user or customer counts are claimed publicly.** Do not assume traction from the existence of auth/saved-product features.

### 3. Why are you selling?
**Seller input required** for the final public sentence. Typical legitimate frames: focus shift; prefer a buyer with distribution; consolidate efforts. Do not invent a dramatic story.

### 4. What does the buyer receive?
Source code and product IP under the purchase agreement; a runnable commerce decision web app; documentation; database migrations; core quality gates; and a staged diligence path. Domains and third-party accounts transfer only if negotiated.

### 5. Is the code proprietary?
It is a **private product codebase** intended to transfer via a commercial agreement. Public open-source relicensing is not implied. Exact ownership/assignment terms are legal closing items.

### 6. What third-party services are required?
Typically: hosting, authentication, database, external product discovery API, payment provider for subscriptions, and optionally an LLM provider for assist surfaces. Exact vendors and transfer steps are shared in diligence (Level 2–3), not as a secrets dump publicly.

### 7. What are the ongoing costs?
**Seller input required** for a monthly estimate. Expect usage-variable discovery and AI costs plus standard SaaS/hosting subscriptions. Buyers should model their own traffic.

### 8. How difficult is deployment?
A technical buyer familiar with modern Next.js apps and SaaS envs can deploy. It is **not** “one-click with zero services.” Expect environment setup, migrations, and provider accounts. Handover runbooks exist for diligence.

### 9. What remains unfinished?
Experimental intelligence modules may remain behind defaults-off flags. GTM, distribution, and proven monetization are unfinished commercial work. Latency optimization vs big-tech shopping UIs remains an operating concern.

### 10. What performance evidence exists?
Public claim: live search works on a deployed demo URL, and cold/first searches can take several seconds because discovery is upstream-dependent. Detailed latency tables and probe artifacts are **NDA / diligence materials**, not public SLAs.

### 11. What is public versus NDA-only?
**Public:** product story, capabilities at a high level, pre-revenue status, limitations, screenshots/demo of the UI.  
**NDA:** repository, detailed architecture, test evidence, env/transfer checklists, credentials process, deeper commercial discussion.

### 12. What support will the seller provide?
**Seller input required.** Recommended public default: time-boxed handover for access transfer and walkthrough — not unpaid perpetual support.

### 13. Can the product be renamed?
Yes, subject to practical engineering/branding work after close. The historical package name may differ from the QuantAI brand; that is a known naming detail, not a blocker.

### 14. Can another discovery provider replace the current shopping API?
Possible in principle, but **not trivial**. Discovery quality and result shape are coupled to the current upstream. Treat replacement as a post-close engineering project unless already built.

### 15. Are there vendor dependencies?
Yes — normal for this class of product (auth, DB, billing, discovery, hosting, optional AI). Migration away from any vendor takes real effort.

### 16. Is the repository clean?
The product is sale-candidate packaged with documentation and gates. Like most real codebases, it is feature-dense. Diligence should review live vs dormant modules rather than assume every file is production-critical.

### 17. What assets must be transferred?
Code/IP, docs, and negotiated items such as domain/DNS, hosting project, auth app, database, billing account, discovery account, and optional AI/rate-limit accounts.

### 18. Are production credentials included?
**Not in the public listing.** Credentials are handled in a controlled closing/transfer process after agreement — never pasted into marketplaces or email threads casually.

### 19. Is there customer or personal data?
Public listing claims no customer book. If any auth/user rows exist in a transferred database, treat them as personal data under privacy law — export/delete/transfer must be handled lawfully. Prefer transferring empty or scrubbed non-production data unless negotiated.

### 20. What is the ideal acquisition structure?
Asset / IP purchase of the software business with escrow, NDA diligence, LOI, then staged credential and account transfer. Exact legal structure: **Seller input required** (counsel).

### 21. Does QuantAI own product inventory?
No.

### 22. Is this a marketplace?
No. Shoppers complete purchases on merchant sites.

### 23. Do you have patents or trademarks?
None claimed in this public kit. **Seller input required** if any filings exist.

### 24. Can I see the code before an NDA?
No. Repository access is Level 3 (signed NDA / serious diligence).
