# Demo Video Script — QuantAI (3–4 minutes)

**Format:** Screen recording of the **live product** (not slides-only)  
**Target length:** 3:00–4:00  
**Voice:** Calm, precise, no hype  
**URL:** Prefer production/staging hostname confirmed for search (commonly `https://quant-ai-app.vercel.app`)  
**Prep:** Warm caches with the three safe searches once before recording  

---

## Safe demo searches
*(use exactly these unless seller substitutes equally safe multi-merchant queries)*

1. `MacBook Pro 14`  
2. `Sony WH-1000XM5`  
3. `corner sofa`  

---

## Statements that must NOT be claimed on camera

- Revenue, profit, ARR, MRR  
- Active user / customer counts  
- “Patented” / “guaranteed best prices”  
- “Fastest shopping AI” / hard SLA latency guarantees  
- “We own the catalog” / “exclusive retailer partners”  
- “All AI layers are live”  
- Specific unverified benchmark numbers  

Allowed honesty: “pre-revenue,” “decision software,” “depends on upstream discovery,” “cold searches can take longer.”

---

## Exact sequence

### Scene 0 — Cold open (0:00–0:20)
**Page:** Homepage `/`  
**On screen:** QuantAI brand + search box  
**Narration:**  
“This is QuantAI — an AI-assisted commerce decision app. It doesn’t own a product catalog. It helps shoppers decide what to buy across multi-merchant offers. Important upfront: QuantAI is pre-revenue. You’re evaluating software and decision IP, not a cash-flow business.”

**Buyer value:** Sets honest frame; avoids diligence landmines.

---

### Scene 1 — First search (0:20–1:10)
**Page:** Submit search `MacBook Pro 14` → results  
**On screen:** Results grid loading then cards with labels  
**Narration:**  
“I’ll run a real search: MacBook Pro 14. QuantAI discovers offers from external shopping sources, then applies a single canonical ranking authority and decision calibration. Watch for clear labels — like BUY, COMPARE, BEST VALUE, or AVOID — instead of a raw unsorted dump.”

**Buyer value:** Proves live product + decision labels.

**If slow:** Do not apologize excessively; say: “First searches depend on upstream discovery and can take several seconds. That’s a known operating characteristic, not hidden.”

---

### Scene 2 — Decision coherence (1:10–1:45)
**Page:** Same results — scroll to decision brief / top recommendation  
**On screen:** Brief aligned with top cards  
**Narration:**  
“The point of QuantAI is coherence. The recommendation surface is meant to agree with the ranked results — one decision system, not conflicting sorts across the page.”

**Buyer value:** Differentiates from badge spam / chatbot wrappers.

---

### Scene 3 — Merchant diversity (1:45–2:15)
**Page:** Same or quickly re-run if needed; highlight multiple merchants  
**On screen:** Multiple store names in top results  
**Narration:**  
“You should also see cross-merchant alternatives. QuantAI is built to preserve useful retailer diversity rather than collapsing everything into a single store list.”

**Buyer value:** Commerce realism for comparison/affiliate strategics.

---

### Scene 4 — Second query + compare (2:15–3:00)
**Pages:** Search `Sony WH-1000XM5` → open compare with 2–3 products  
**On screen:** Compare tray / compare panel  
**Narration:**  
“Second live query: Sony WH-1000XM5. Now I’ll compare a few alternatives. QuantAI’s compare flow is part of the same decision experience — helping the shopper choose between close options, not just browse.”

**Buyer value:** Engagement depth beyond search SERP.

---

### Scene 5 — Continuity + monetization foundation (3:00–3:30)
**Pages:** Saved products (demo account) → Pricing `/pricing`  
**On screen:** Saved list (demo data) + public plans  
**Narration:**  
“Authenticated users can save products for follow-through. Pricing and billing foundations are in the product for subscription monetization. Again — this listing does not claim verified revenue today.”

**Buyer value:** Shows path to monetization without fake traction.

---

### Scene 6 — Optional third query beat (only if time) (3:30–3:45)
**Page:** `corner sofa` results briefly  
**Narration:**  
“One more category — furniture — to show this isn’t a single-vertical scraper.”

**Buyer value:** Category flexibility.

---

### Scene 7 — Close / CTA (final 15–20s)
**Page:** Homepage or pricing  
**Narration:**  
“If you’re a strategic or technical buyer looking for a commerce decision engine — with ranking discipline, calibrated labels, and a runnable app — request an intro call. Serious diligence continues under NDA: deeper architecture, quality gates, and a structured asset-transfer plan. Thanks for watching.”

**Buyer value:** Clear next step; boundaries respected.
